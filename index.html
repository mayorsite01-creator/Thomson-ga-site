Everything Thomson Offers

City Services & Resources

The City of Thomson provides a comprehensive range of municipal services to every resident and business in McDuffie County. Every department is committed to professionalism, responsiveness, and excellence.

Utilities & Infrastructure

Thomson operates its own water, sewer, and sanitation systems — delivering reliable services to every address in the city.

Water & Sewer Services

Clean, safe drinking water and wastewater treatment for all Thomson households and businesses. Online billing available 24/7.

Sanitation & Solid Waste

Weekly curbside collection of household waste and recyclables. Bulk pickup scheduling and special disposal services available.

Streets & Public Works

Road maintenance, pothole repair, stormwater management, street lighting, and sidewalk upkeep across all Thomson city streets.

Building Permits & Inspections

Residential and commercial building permits, code enforcement, and inspection scheduling for all construction and renovation projects in Thomson.

Community & Civic Services

Thomson invests in the quality of life for every resident through robust public safety, parks, and community programming.

Police Department

The Thomson Police Department provides 24/7 law enforcement services, community policing programs, and emergency response throughout the city. Non-emergency: (706) 595-2081.

Fire & Emergency Services

Thomson Fire Department delivers rapid-response fire suppression, rescue, and emergency medical services to protect every Thomson household and business.

Parks & Recreation

Thomson's parks system offers athletic fields, playgrounds, walking trails, picnic facilities, and community event spaces for residents of all ages.

Planning & Zoning

Land use planning, zoning ordinances, variance requests, and development review for residential and commercial projects in Thomson and McDuffie County.

About Mayor Darby

Rooted in Thomson.
Committed to McDuffie County.

Mayor Ed Darby serves the City of Thomson, Georgia with a deep sense of responsibility to the community, the residents, and the legacy of McDuffie County's proud seat of government. His leadership is rooted in the belief that local government must be transparent, responsive, and genuinely committed to the wellbeing of every person it serves.

With years of experience in public service and civic leadership, Mayor Darby understands that the strength of a city comes not from its buildings or budgets, but from the character of its people and the quality of leadership that represents them. From economic development initiatives designed to bring good jobs to McDuffie County to infrastructure investments that improve daily life for Thomson families, his priorities are clear and his approach is hands-on.

Under Mayor Darby's leadership, Thomson is investing in its future — modernizing city services, expanding digital access to government resources, and strengthening the partnerships between City Hall and the community organizations, businesses, and residents who make Thomson the city it is.

He believes that great city government is defined by its responsiveness: when a Thomson resident calls, emails, or walks through the door of City Hall, they deserve to be heard, respected, and helped — every single time.

Mayor's Strategic Priorities

Economic development & job creation in McDuffie County
Infrastructure investment — roads, water, and city facilities
Public safety & community-oriented policing
Modern digital services accessible to every Thomson resident
Transparent, accountable governance for the whole community
